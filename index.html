A FEW THINGS, UNRAVELED

Good
to know.

Care starts with knowing
what your fabric needs.

Is this a lint roller?+

No. A lint roller lifts loose surface debris using adhesive. A fabric shaver trims raised fabric pills. It does not remove stains or repair damaged fibers.

Can I use it on my favorite sweater?+

Check your sweater’s care instructions and the shaver manual first. Test an inconspicuous area. Avoid loose knits, loose threads, decorations and delicate fabrics unless specifically allowed.

What is the difference between the finishes?+

Ivory is the warm off-white finish; Charcoal is the dark grey finish. The color choice changes the appearance, not the intended function. Lighting and screen settings affect how colors appear.

Is it rechargeable?+

Yes. The supplied product references show USB-C charging and a digital battery display. Charging and care instructions should be followed as set out in the supplied manual.
